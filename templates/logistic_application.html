# 1. Problem Context

Business Problem Being Analyzed

What problem is being analyzed? Customer churn: the abandonment of the service by subscribers of a telecommunications company.

What do the data represent? 600 historical client records with their permanence time in the company and whether they left (churn = 1) or stayed (churn = 0).

Independent variable (X): Time as a client in months — tenure_months. Target variable (Y): churn.

Two possible classes: 0 = Se queda (Stay) 1 = Se va (Churn)

What does the model classify? It attempts to classify whether a client with a given tenure is likely to leave the service (probability of churn).
