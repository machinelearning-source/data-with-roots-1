3. Calculation method

Distance, assignment, and centroid update

For every record, the Euclidean distance to each centroid is calculated:

d = sqrt((distance_km - centroid_km)^2 + (duration_min - centroid_min)^2)
  1. Assign the record to the centroid with the smallest distance.
  2. Calculate the new centroid as the mean of the coordinates assigned to that cluster.
  3. Repeat the process exactly three times.

The application reproduces the same calculations deterministically, making every distance, assignment, and update auditable.
