Use Case 1

Medical Diagnosis with AI

Application of Machine Learning for automated lesion detection and early pathology diagnosis from clinical imaging.

Problem Context

Early clinical diagnosis is vital for successful oncology intervention, particularly in cutaneous malignancies like melanoma. While experienced dermatologists can identify malignant lesions, widespread shortages of specialists and human fatigue often lead to delayed diagnoses in remote areas.

The core challenge is: How to automate the early detection and segmentation of malignant skin lesions from dermatoscopic medical imagery, shortening turnaround times while maintaining dermatologist-level diagnostic sensitivity.

🏥

ML Paradigm

Supervised Learning - Image Classification

Deep Learning CNN Transfer Learning

Data Utilized

  • High-resolution dermatoscopic skin lesion photographs.
  • Clinical patient metadata (age, sex, anatomical lesion location, genetic history).
  • Extracted morphological features: Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Color variegation, Diameter (ABCD rule).
  • Histopathologically confirmed biopsy labels (benign vs. malignant).

What the Model Predicts

  • Binary Classification: Whether the examined lesion is benign or malignant.
  • Confidence Score: Calibrated probability metric associated with the diagnosis.
  • Pathology Subtype: Distinguishes between melanoma, basal cell carcinoma, and benign nevi.

Potential Benefits

Dramatically reduces preliminary screening turnaround from weeks to seconds, facilitates mass telemedicine screening in underserved rural clinics, provides dermatologists with an objective second diagnostic opinion, and significantly enhances early survival rates through timely intervention.
