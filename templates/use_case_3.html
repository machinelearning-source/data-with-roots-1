Use Case 3

Retail Demand Forecasting

Machine Learning to optimize supply chain inventory replenishment and maximize omnichannel customer fulfillment.

Problem Context

Retail chains constantly struggle to balance warehouse stock: holding insufficient inventory causes stockouts and lost revenue, whereas holding excess inventory causes capital tie-up, warehouse congestion, and inventory depreciation through spoilage or obsolescence.

The core challenge is: How to accurately forecast future SKU-level consumer demand across dynamic regional markets to optimize purchase orders, reduce waste, and maximize customer satisfaction.

🛒

ML Paradigm

Supervised Learning - Time Series Regression

Time Series XGBoost LSTM

Data Utilized

  • Granular point-of-sale (POS) transactional history by product and store.
  • Historical pricing dynamics, marketing campaigns, and seasonal discounts.
  • Calendar and holiday variables (paydays, regional festivals, weekends).
  • External macro variables (regional weather forecasts, local foot-traffic data).
  • Competitor price indexes and supply chain lead times.

What the Model Predicts

  • Expected Unit Sales: Projected volume of units sold per item across daily and weekly intervals.
  • Safety Stock Thresholds: Dynamic reorder points adapted to current demand volatility.
  • Stockout Warnings: Predictive alerts flagging SKUs at risk of running out before scheduled deliveries.

Potential Benefits

Reduces storage holding costs and product write-downs by 25% to 35%, significantly curtails grocery food spoilage, elevates on-shelf availability to over 98%, and optimizes working capital allocation across distribution centers.
