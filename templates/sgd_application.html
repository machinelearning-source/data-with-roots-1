# 1. Problem Context

Business Problem Being Analyzed

What problem is being analyzed? Credit risk assessment: determining whether a loan applicant is likely to default on their payments.

What do the data represent? 600 loan applicants with 4 numeric features and their assigned risk class.

Independent variables (X = 4): income_usd (monthly income), loan_amount_usd (requested amount), credit_score (300–850), months_employed (employment history in months).

Target variable (Y): high_risk

Two possible classes: 0 = Low Risk (Bajo riesgo) 1 = High Risk (Alto riesgo)

What does the model classify? It classifies each applicant as High Risk or Low Risk of default based on all four variables entered by the user.
